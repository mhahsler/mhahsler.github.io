I am a Clinical Associate Professor of [Computer Science](https://www.smu.edu/Lyle/Academics/Departments/CS) 
at the Bobby B. Lyle School of Engineering, SMU.
I serve as an associate editor of the [Journal of Statistical Software](https://www.jstatsoft.org/).

My research interests lie in the intersection of machine learning, statistical methods, and combinatorial optimization with applications in 
**artificial intelligence, data mining, and data science** (see [Research page]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) for details). An important goal is to support **reproducible research** by publishing high-quality software 
(see [Software page]({{ '/software' | relative_url }})) along with all theoretical 
results. 
My research team and I maintain over 15 widely used
[R](https://r-project.org/)&nbsp;packages, including
[arules](https://github.com/mhahsler/arules) (also as [arulespy](https://pypi.org/project/arulespy/)),
[dbscan](https://github.com/mhahsler/dbscan),
[pomdp](https://github.com/mhahsler/pomdp),
[seriation](https://github.com/mhahsler/seriation), 
[stream](https://github.com/mhahsler/stream), 
and [recommenderlab](https://github.com/mhahsler/recommenderlab).
Read more in my [short Bio]({{ '/docs/hahsler_short_bio.pdf' | relative_url }}) or my [full academic CV.]({{ '/docs/hahsler_cv_full.pdf' | relative_url }})
