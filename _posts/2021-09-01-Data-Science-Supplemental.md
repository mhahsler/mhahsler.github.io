I collaborate with [Prof. Usama El Shamy](https://www.smu.edu/Lyle/Academics/Departments/CEE/People/Faculty/ElShamyUsama) on the NSF-funded project Data Science Supplemental: [Evaluation of Liquefaction Potential of Saturated Granular Soils Under Partial Drainage Conditions](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=1728612) (Supplement to Award # 1728612). The goal of the supplemental funding is to use AI and data mining to learn from large scale simulation data.
