The R examples used in my course CS 5/7331 Data Mining are now 
compiled into a [downloadable PDF book](https://mhahsler.github.io/Introduction_to_Data_Mining_R_Examples/book/R-Companion-Data-Mining.pdf) 
(DOI 
[10.6084/m9.figshare.26750404](http://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26750404))
and an [interactive online book](https://mhahsler.github.io/Introduction_to_Data_Mining_R_Examples/book/). 



The companion book is the official R Code for the popular textbook [Introduction to Data Mining](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~kumar001/dmbook/index.php) by Tan et al.
