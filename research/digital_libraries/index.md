<blockquote>
      Digital libraries are organizations that provide the
      resources, including the specialized staff, to select,
      structure, offer intellectual access to, interpret,
      distribute, preserve the integrity of, and ensure the
      persistence over time of collections of digital works so that
      they are readily and economically available for use by a
      defined community or set of communities.
</blockquote>
<div align="right">
      Digital Library Federation (DLF) 1998
</div>

<p>
      For more definitions see <a href=
      "http://www.clevelandmemory.org/SpecColl/cdl/DLdef.html">Defining
      Digital Library, Cleveland Digital Library</a>
</p>

  Prototype developed for the University Library

        ePub<sup>WU</sup> -
	Electronic Publications (operational 2002-2010). 
	The system features an OAI-compliant publication platform 
	for acquiring and managing the university's 
	digital PhD theses and working papers.
	We collected more than 750 documents in 8 years. In 
	2010 the system was replaced by an Eprints instance.
